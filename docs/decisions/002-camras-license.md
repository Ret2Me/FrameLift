# ADR 002: CAMRAS publication license

Status: **publication blocked pending recorded review**.

The dataset-specific CAMRAS page states CC BY 4.0, while the general public-data
terms use CC BY-NC 4.0 unless otherwise specified. The dataset-specific notice
may be the intended exception; it is not silently generalized to every CAMRAS
artifact. Store the exact notice, URL, access time, hash, and attribution for
each manifest snapshot. No derivative publication is enabled until scope is
reviewed and recorded.

## Draft inquiry (not sent)

Please confirm which license applies to files and derived manifests under
`data.camras.nl/satnogs/`, and whether redistribution of derived research
metadata and small validation fixtures is permitted with attribution. We are
considering a scientific publication and reproducible benchmark.
